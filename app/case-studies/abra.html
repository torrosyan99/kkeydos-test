About Abra

Abra is committed to changing how the world moves money. It was the world's first blockchain-based peer-to-peer money transfer network. Users can invest in, buy, sell, or hold over 100 cryptocurrencies, send money, and manage their crypto portfolios.

The challenge.

Abra wanted to streamline the trading experience on their crypto exchange and improve the experience for 2M users across 150 countries.

The project required engineers with experience in secure financial platforms, mobile development, quality assurance, and cryptocurrency.

Abra chose a staff augmentation engagement to bring that expertise into its team.

A better trading experience.
For 2M users across 150 countries.

The solution.

Abra chose a staff augmentation engagement model, bringing in software engineers with Android, Java, Scala, and iOS expertise.

All technologies used.

  • React
  • Android
  • Java
  • iOS
  • Kotlin

The outcome.

Over the 4-year engagement, the team:

  • Improved test automation and coverage.
  • Enhanced QA and user risk assessment processes.
  • Built a US domestic wires and ACH module to enhance and maintain liquidity options.
  • Developed an ETFs and stock trading module.
