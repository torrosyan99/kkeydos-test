Services Backend Development

Boost digital performance with our backend development solutions.

KKEYDOS builds secure, scalable backend systems for web and mobile applications. We bring together business logic, connected data, APIs, and integrations to keep your product running reliably as it grows.

From cloud platforms and real-time services to AI-ready data workflows, our backend development team helps you build the foundations for your next release and long-term growth.

Our WorkContact Us

Backend development that supports your business.

Your application needs a dependable foundation. We build the services, data flows, and integrations behind the experience, with clear architecture that your team can maintain.

Our backend development services include:

  • Custom server-side development built around your business rules, users, and growth plans.
  • API-first architecture connecting web and mobile products, third-party tools, and AI services.
  • Database design and management for consistent data, efficient queries, and reliable scaling.
  • Application performance with caching, background processing, and real-time data flows.
  • Security-focused engineering covering authentication, permissions, and data protection.
  • System audits and migration to modernize your platform while maintaining existing workflows.

Expertise for complex application requirements.

As your product grows, its backend needs to handle more than incoming requests. New features, larger datasets, and external systems all introduce decisions that affect reliability.

We work across Python, Node.js, PHP, and modern databases to build the right foundation for your application. Architecture, authentication, API design, and deployment are considered together.

We also plan how your backend will be deployed, monitored, and maintained. Our team can support CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and DevOps practices that make releases easier to manage.

Whether you are starting from scratch or improving an existing platform, we help your team deliver the next release and prepare for what comes after it.

Backend development: your questions answered.

We assess your current system and plan the migration around its dependencies. Staged releases, data synchronization, and rollback procedures help limit disruption; the downtime target is agreed after the technical review.

The estimate depends on business logic, integrations, data volume, security needs, and the condition of any existing code. Share your requirements and we will outline scope, delivery milestones, and a project-specific estimate.

We combine secure authentication, access controls, input validation, encrypted connections, dependency reviews, and monitoring. The controls are selected for the application’s data and operating environment.

There is no single best stack for every product. We compare options such as Python, Node.js, and PHP against your workloads, existing systems, team skills, and long-term maintenance needs.

A backend team builds the server-side logic, databases, APIs, and integrations that power an application. It also supports deployment, performance tuning, security, and ongoing maintenance.

We can work on a defined project, provide a dedicated delivery team, or add backend specialists to your existing team. Scope and responsibilities are agreed before work starts.

Yes. We can help with monitoring, bug fixes, dependency updates, performance improvements, and new features through an agreed support arrangement.
