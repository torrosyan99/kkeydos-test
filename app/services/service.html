A practical guide to building a reliable backend.

How do you choose a backend technology?

Steps:

  1. Map the product features, users, and expected workloads.
  2. Identify API, data, and integration requirements.
  3. Compare frameworks against your team’s experience.
  4. Review hosting, maintenance, and operating costs.
  5. Validate the choice with a focused technical prototype.
How do you design a scalable backend?

Steps:

  1. Separate business responsibilities into clear modules.
  2. Define authentication, permissions, and trust boundaries.
  3. Model your data and measure common queries.
  4. Plan caching, background jobs, and scaling limits.
  5. Test the system under realistic load before release.
How do you connect a frontend to an API?

Steps:

  1. Agree on request formats, responses, and error handling.
  2. Document REST or GraphQL interfaces for the client team.
  3. Configure authentication and allowed origins.
  4. Integrate the web or mobile client against a test environment.
  5. Verify loading states, retries, and failed requests end to end.
How do you handle user authentication?

Steps:

  1. Choose an established identity provider or proven framework.
  2. Define account roles and permissions for each workflow.
  3. Use secure password hashing and session handling.
  4. Protect account recovery and sensitive operations.
  5. Review access controls with automated tests and audit logs.
How do you maintain a backend after launch?

Steps:

  1. Monitor availability, response times, and error rates.
  2. Centralize logs so incidents can be investigated.
  3. Schedule backups and test recovery procedures.
  4. Keep dependencies and infrastructure up to date.
  5. Use production feedback to prioritize improvements.
