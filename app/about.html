About KKEYDOS

We partner with businesses to build powerful digital products that simplify challenges, unlock opportunities, and accelerate growth. We make technology work for business.

Our Story

From a small team with a big idea to a technology partner for growing businesses.

Founded in Hyderabad in 2018, KKEYDOS began by helping businesses turn their ideas into digital products. Today, we build and scale web, mobile, SaaS, AI, eCommerce, CRM, and enterprise solutions for clients across India and international markets.

We started with a vision.
Today, we’re building what comes next.

Since then...

We’ve delivered 280+ digital products for 177 clients worldwide.

From startups to established businesses, our work has helped companies turn ideas into working digital products and build technology that supports their growth.

How KKEYDOS grew over the years

2018

The Beginning

KKEYDOS was founded in Hyderabad with a simple vision: help businesses turn their ideas into practical digital solutions.

2019

Building Our Foundation

We focused on building our development capabilities and delivering our first generation of websites, applications, and business software.

2020

Expanding Our Capabilities

KKEYDOS expanded its technology capabilities and began working on more complex digital products for businesses across different industries.

2021

Growing Beyond Projects

We moved beyond individual development projects and started taking on larger, more structured digital transformation requirements.

2022

Reaching Global Markets

Our work expanded to international clients, strengthening KKEYDOS as a technology partner for businesses beyond India.

2023

Scaling Digital Products

We delivered an increasing range of digital products and strengthened our capabilities across business applications and scalable platforms.

2024

Building for Business Growth

KKEYDOS focused increasingly on solutions designed around business operations, automation, scalability, and long-term product growth.

2025

Moving Toward AI & SaaS

We began placing greater focus on AI-powered solutions, SaaS products, automation, and next-generation business applications.

2026

Building What’s Next

Today, KKEYDOS has delivered 280+ products for 177+ clients worldwide, with a growing focus on building scalable digital products and technology solutions for the next generation of businesses.
