Frequently Asked Questions

General

KKEYDOS designs and develops digital products for businesses, from web and mobile applications to AI solutions, SaaS platforms, and enterprise systems. Our teams support discovery, design, engineering, quality assurance, deployment, and ongoing improvement.

We help you add the skills your roadmap needs, build a dedicated delivery team, or outsource a complete project. We agree on the scope, the required expertise, and how our engineers will work with your existing team before getting started.

Working with an extended engineering team gives you access to specialist skills and additional delivery capacity. Clear communication, agreed working hours, and shared delivery processes help the team collaborate effectively.

Our services include custom software development, web and mobile applications, AI and automation, SaaS development, cloud and DevOps, API integrations, UX/UI design, and quality assurance.

Industries

We build software for healthcare, fintech, e-commerce, education, logistics, real estate, and other industries. We align each solution with the workflows, integrations, and security requirements of the business.

Engagement Models

Staff augmentation adds engineers to your team under your direction. A dedicated team provides an ongoing group of specialists working on your roadmap. Project outsourcing gives us responsibility for an agreed scope and delivery plan, with regular reviews and progress updates.

Technologies / Solutions

Depending on your project, we can provide frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile, cloud, AI, data, and QA expertise, alongside designers and delivery specialists.
