How our vision became reality

2009

BairesDev was founded. The vision to offer top-quality technical talent with time zone alignment, anywhere in the world was born.

2011

Expanded to Brazil, Colombia, and Mexico and quickly became one of the fastest-growing companies in Latin America.

2012

Entered the US market, began operations in San Francisco.

2014

Acquired our 100th client.

2015

Received over 100k applications and reached a 300-employee headcount.

2016

Achieved a 96% client retention rate.

2018

CEO and co-founder, Nacho De Marco, joined the Forbes Technology Council, an invitation-only community for world-class CIOs, CTOs, and top technology leaders.

2019

Created our Giveback Program to promote social responsibility.

2020

Executed 27 projects to accelerate innovation during the covid pandemic. Achieved a 70% growth rate.

2021

Delivered over 1,250 projects across 100+ industry sectors.

2022

Vetted over 1.5 million candidates. Over 4,000 specialists on board from more than 40 countries.

2023

Named the 2nd-fastest-growing private company in Silicon Valley. Winner of the Most Innovative Company award for its accomplishments in AI and more.

2024

CEO and Co-Founder Nacho De Marco spoke at SXSW, WebSummit, WEF, and Stanford. He was also featured on Forbes, and won the EY Entrepreneur of the Year award.

2025

Harvard published a case study on BairesDev's culture and growth. CEO and co-founder Nacho De Marco spoke at the World Economic Annual Meeting at Davos for the first time.

2026

BairesDev became a World Economic Forum Associated Partner, deepening its impact on global tech and the future of work as part of the Centre for the New Economy and Society.
